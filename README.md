# Vinícius Lisboa

**How do we know there's *someone* on the other side?**

One question, approached from two opposite directions.

**From the outside** — At [Hummand](https://www.hummand.com.br), I build
infrastructure to verify humanity: deepfake-resistant liveness detection,
proof of personhood, and verifiable identity for both people and the AI
agents acting on their behalf. GovTech, in production, Brazil.

**From the inside** — As an independent researcher, I study machine
consciousness, cognition, and AI welfare — building open tools to
evaluate sentience indicators in real models. Not claiming machines are
conscious; not claiming they aren't. Working on how we'd tell.

## Building

- **[Hummand](https://www.hummand.com.br)** — digital identity and proof
  of humanity as infrastructure for the public sector
- **[The Alert](https://thealert.io)** — uptime monitoring and
  observability platform
- **[Nameplate](https://nameplate.com.br)** — live monitoring and
  forecasting of renewable energy curtailment in Northeast Brazil, built
  on open grid data (ONS) — a student project I mentor as a teacher
- **[Canal Zero](https://canalzero.hummand.com.br)** — anonymous school
  violence reporting channel for Paraíba's public school network, built
  by students under my mentorship
